Full methodology
  1. 01 Assessment Review identity, endpoints, email, backups, network, and operating reality.
  2. 02 Roadmap Match the operating model to the environment — fit, not a ranking ladder.
  3. 03 Implementation Documented credentials you own. Controls sized to the model we matched.
