Identity & Access

Answers · credential theft

Control who can access business systems, how access is verified, and how accounts are changed or removed over time.

Questions the assessment should answer

  • Is MFA appropriate and enforced where required?
  • Are privileged accounts separated and reviewed?
  • Does offboarding remove access consistently?

How protection works

Assessment → Roadmap → Implementation → Continuous

Full methodology
  1. 01 Assessment Review identity, endpoints, email, backups, network, and operating reality.
  2. 02 Roadmap Match the operating model to the environment — fit, not a ranking ladder.
  3. 03 Implementation Documented credentials you own. Controls sized to the model we matched.
  4. 04 Continuous Day-to-day support and the DE Security Foundation are included at every tier; detection, response, recovery, and governance deepen with the plan.
