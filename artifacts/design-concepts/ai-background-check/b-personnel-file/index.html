SECTION 1

Who owns the account

Personal AI works for the person. Business AI works for the business.

When someone uses AI on a personal account, the chats, the files and the history belong to that person, not your company. When they leave, it stays in their personal account, where your company can't reach it. Business AI is set up under your company: your company controls the data, decides who uses it, and can switch off access the day someone leaves.

SECTION 4

Regulatory flags

Already regulated? This matters more.

  • Healthcare

    Patient information in the wrong AI tool can be a HIPAA problem. Any AI tool that handles patient information needs a signed business associate agreement (BAA), and only some business plans offer one.

  • Finance and insurance

    GLBA and the FTC Safeguards Rule set how you protect client financial data and oversee the vendors who handle it, AI included.

  • Defense contractors

    Controlled unclassified information (CUI) has no place in consumer AI. CMMC assessors will ask.
