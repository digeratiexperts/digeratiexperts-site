Page-flip engine — turning, not crossfading

Prototype · zero dependencies · drag a page corner, or use the arrow keys

Drag from the outer third of either page and the leaf tracks your pointer; let go past a third of the way and it completes, short of that it springs back. The shading, the fold near the spine and the shadow cast on the page beneath are all driven off the same turn angle. Under prefers-reduced-motion there is no rotation at all — pages just change.
