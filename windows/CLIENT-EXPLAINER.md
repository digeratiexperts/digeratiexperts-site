# What DE Tech Tool changes on your device

*A short explainer for our clients. Digerati Experts runs this tool on your Windows devices when we set
them up, take them over from another provider, or work on them during service. Everything below is what
it may touch — nothing else.*

## Why we run it

The tool sets your device up to a consistent, secure standard and records what it checked and changed,
so the work is verifiable and repeatable. Your technician sees the same record you can ask for.

## What it may change

| Area | What happens |
|---|---|
| User accounts and sign-in | Your accounts are set up for managed sign-in, with clear naming; existing files and profiles are kept in place |
| Device encryption | Full-disk encryption is turned on, and a recovery key is escrowed safely so you are never locked out |
| Security protection | Your security tools are installed, enabled and set to the right protection mode; conflicting tools are resolved |
| Standard applications | Your organisation's standard apps are installed from trusted sources; anything not needed is identified before removal |
| Browser settings | Safe defaults, managed extensions and the right update policy for your browsers |
| Device name and branding | The device is named to your organisation's convention and shows your company's sign-in screen |
| Network and printing | Site network settings, Wi-Fi profiles and printers are set up |
| Backup and support tools | Your backup and remote support tools are installed and pointed at the right accounts |
| Windows updates | Update settings are aligned so the device stays current through your management platform |
| Security baseline | A hardened Windows baseline is applied and checked item by item |
| Work needed before sign-off | Anything the tool cannot complete safely is listed for your technician, never forced |

Every change is planned first, applied, verified, and recorded. If something does not verify, the tool
rolls it back and reports it rather than leaving the device half-configured.

## What it does not do

- It does not read your documents, email or browsing.
- It does not store your sign-in credentials; anything sensitive is entered at the time of the work and
  kept out of reports and records.
- It does not make changes you cannot see: everything it does appears in the record of the work.
- It does not run itself on its own — a technician runs it, or your management platform schedules the
  agreed work.

## What is recorded

A record of the checks, changes and verification for your device: what was done, when, and which
technician did it. Personal details are kept to what the work needs. Records of raw device detail are
kept for 13 months; the audit record of the work is kept for 6 years.

## When the service ends

Ask us and we will remove DE management from the device in an orderly way: DE tools come off, your data
stays yours, and you are left with an administrator account you control.

## Questions

Your Digerati Experts technician can show you the record for any device at any time — just ask.
