Common questions

Straight answers.

What is your best service?

There isn't a universally "best" package. ProActive is four operating models — IT, Office, Business, and Enterprise — matched to users, devices, locations, infrastructure, security, compliance, and whether you need fully or co-managed coverage.

How do I choose the right plan?

Every fully managed or co-managed plan starts with a required Cyber Risk Assessment. User count is a signal, never the sole criterion. After the assessment we match IT, Office, Business, or Enterprise. We don't start with a package and pile on add-ons.

Can I customize the solutions?

Yes — within the operating model that fits. Scope is matched to your environment, and we offer fully managed or co-managed coverage to fit your existing IT structure. Specific needs can also be handled through standalone services.

Do you work with businesses outside Arizona?

Yes, through Ready-to-Work IT. For businesses under 30 users, we buy the devices, set them up, test them, and ship them ready to use, and we provide per-user licenses like Microsoft 365. It's built for business owners and IT departments, in Arizona and beyond.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use enterprise-grade controls and documented security protocols, with monitoring matched to your plan — 24/7 SOC/MDR on Business and Enterprise. We help Arizona businesses prepare for HIPAA, PCI DSS, SOC 2, and cyber-insurance reviews, with clear ownership of credentials, policies, and evidence.
