The Cyber Risk Assessment

Every engagement starts the same way: DE reads your environment before recommending anything. This page explains what that involves.

What it reviews

The assessment reads the parts of your environment where most incidents actually start:

  • Identity and access, including MFA coverage
  • Endpoints and devices
  • Email security
  • Backups and recovery
  • The foundational security controls around all of it

How it runs

You book a time. DE reviews the environment and returns findings a business owner can act on: prioritized, with business-impact context and right-sized recommendations. No obligation, and you hear back within one business day.

What happens after

The findings stand on their own; you can act on them with anyone. When the fit is right, they become the starting map for a ProActive operating model, matched to your users, devices, compliance needs, and whether you want fully or co-managed coverage.
