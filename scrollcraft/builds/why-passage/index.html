The passage

Control before complexity.

Six stages, in this order, every time. Later phases depend on earlier prerequisites, and an exception is only ever granted in writing.

01

Legal and authority

MSA, Order Form and SOW. The authorized decision maker, the contacts, the access authority. Clarifies who can approve what, and what DE actually owns.

02

Assessment and discovery

Cyber risk, site, network, endpoint, SaaS and compliance discovery, scoped to the engagement. Finds risk, waste, unsupported conditions and hidden dependencies.

03

Environment intake

Admin access review. Users, devices, systems, vendors, data, baseline documentation. Creates visibility and operational context.

04

Stabilization

Immediate risks, access gaps, unsupported conditions and backup concerns are addressed or planned. Stops unknown problems becoming managed-service assumptions.

05

Security foundation

Identity, endpoint, email and collaboration, and site-level controls reach approved minimum standards. Establishes the security baseline.

06

Activate and expand

Covered services move into normal operations. Infrastructure, workplace, then governance follow. Reactive cleanup becomes intentional management.

Before you choose us

The parts most providers leave out of the brochure.

We may pause your work
When required access, approvals, standards or prerequisites are incomplete, DE can pause, defer, limit or reschedule. Sequencing is not negotiable, because it is what makes everything after it true.
We may say no
You can accept risk. You cannot require DE to violate its standards, or hold DE responsible for an outcome while preventing the controls that achieve it.
A user is not unlimited devices
Coverage is defined by the Order Form. Extra endpoints, shared systems, specialty devices and servers may need separate approval and pricing.
Standalone stays standalone
A single role does not silently inherit full MSP, SOC, backup, network, compliance or helpdesk coverage. You get the role you bought and its documented dependencies.
We are not your HR or finance department
DE runs the technology around people and money. Employment, payroll, benefits, tax and legal decisions stay with you and your qualified providers.

Every one of these is published because a boundary you can read is worth more than a promise you cannot verify. Unsupported conditions, exceptions and material risks are documented rather than hidden.
