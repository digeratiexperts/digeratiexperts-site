Direction 01 · the base register

Calm Engineered Confidence

A high-end advisory practice that happens to have exceptional technical capability. Calm is the premium signal, and calm is also what the buyer arriving from an insurance form or a breach scare actually needs.

Drawn from
FGS Global, Aman, Studio MK27, Snøhetta, a little Kümmerlein
Ground
Warm paper. Never a dark field as the default. Depth comes from white space, not from darkness.
Type
Enormous disciplined grotesque for statements; a serif for body, because serif on paper reads advisory rather than SaaS.
Containers
Almost none. Hairline rules and space do the separating. No card with a border, radius and shadow stamped on every block.
Colour
One accent, used perhaps three times a page. No gradient, no glow, no second accent.
Imagery
Code-built or real photography only. Nothing generated, no abstract particle fields.
Voice
Human language. Declarative sentences that would survive being read aloud to a client.
Frame A Flagship opening screen · Calm Engineered Confidence
Cyber Risk Assessment

Your environment isn't broken. It's been drifting.

Digerati Experts maps the people, devices, cloud services, vendors and risks behind your business. Then it gives every part an owner, a boundary and a direction.

Start with a Cyber Risk Assessment

Pick a time. No obligation. We recommend a fit only after we have looked.

Which is usually why you're here: an insurance form with hard questions, a message that almost worked, a rule you now have to meet, a vendor nobody owns, or a renewal you're not sure about.

What it buys

  • Nothing in the category looks like this, because the category is uniformly dark
  • Calm is legible instantly, before a word is read
  • Cheapest of the three to execute and the easiest to keep consistent
  • Accessibility and contrast get easier, not harder

What it costs

  • Restraint is unforgiving: every spacing and type error is visible
  • It departs from design/BRAND.md, which currently mandates a dark ground
  • It needs real copy discipline — filler collapses it immediately
  • No atmosphere to hide behind if a section has nothing to say
  • The mark needs a paper-ground lockup. The current one is light-on-dark and disappears here; the frames use a one-colour ink reduction as a stand-in
Direction 02 · the product register

Precision Product Company

Where the eight blocks, the cadence, the three ways in and the assessment live. Structure made beautiful rather than decorated — the opposite of a fake dashboard dropped onto a marketing page.

Drawn from
Teenage Engineering, Tailscale, Stripe, Pentagram
Ground
White, harder and cooler than the base register, so a technical surface announces itself.
Grid
Visible and hard. Hairlines are structural, not ornamental — the grid is the diagram.
Type
Mono for every label, number and unit. Grotesque for names. Serif only for the explanatory line.
Evidence
Real interface, real records, real structure. No invented numbers, telemetry or certifications — that is one of the refusals.
Colour
The accent marks state and nothing else: what is continuous, what is next, what is at risk.
Voice
Almost no persuasion. Name the thing, say what it answers, stop.

What it buys

  • The eight blocks finally read as one model rather than eight tiles
  • Technical credibility without a single glowing threat particle
  • A grammar that extends cleanly to Cloud Edge / SASE, Threadline, Switchboard and the marketplace
  • Works at phone width, because a grid degrades gracefully and a diorama does not

What it costs

  • Demands real product evidence — screens, records, actual structure
  • Can read cold if it escapes onto the emotional pages; it must stay on technical surfaces
  • Every number on screen has to be true and sourced, or it comes off
Direction 03 · the motion layer

Bright Cinematic Story

The scroll experience survives the reset. What changes is that every movement now has to mean something: problem → exposure → clarity → plan → protected operating state, over the two registers above rather than inside a dark world of its own.

Drawn from
Lando Norris (OFF+BRAND), Scout Motors
Rides on
Directions 01 and 02. It has no palette of its own and never introduces a dark immersive universe.
Rule
One movement, one meaning. If a transition does not change what the visitor knows, it is deleted.
Transformation
The same elements change state across the beats. Nothing appears from nowhere and nothing is atmosphere.
Conversion
From Scout: the assessment stays reachable throughout. The story never buries the decision.
Forbidden
Glowing points, drifting particles, tiny labels over artwork, copy that merely translates as it scrolls.
Fallback
Reduced motion, no WebGL and phones get the same five beats as a document, not a blank screen.
Frame C The five beats · code-built state changes, shown static

One environment, five states.

Scroll drives the transformation
Beat 01 · problem Everything works. Just not as one. Seven parts, each fine on its own, none of them joined.
Beat 02 · exposure Nobody is steering the whole thing. The dependencies appear, and so do the exceptions nobody owns.
Beat 03 · clarity One owner for the whole environment. The same parts, now aligned. The gaps close and the exceptions go out.
Beat 04 · plan Assess. Understand. Design. Order arrives: what gets fixed first, and who owns each boundary.
Beat 05 · operating One environment. One cadence. One boundary closes around all of it, and it keeps running.

Why this is not the thing you rejected. The old world was atmosphere: glowing points drifting in a dark room, which looked like something happening and communicated nothing. These are the same seven elements in five states, on paper, and each transition says one thing you could repeat afterwards. Shown static here because that is the honest test — if the beats do not read as stills, motion will not save them.

What it buys

  • The ambition survives, with the failure mode removed
  • A visitor can describe what they saw, which is the whole point of a story
  • Reads as a document when motion is off — no blank screens, ever again

What it costs

  • The most expensive layer to build and the one that must be scroll-tested at every speed
  • Discipline: the moment a movement stops meaning something it has to be cut
  • It cannot rescue a weak base — build 01 first

Two things I cannot decide for you

This reset conflicts with the brand document. design/BRAND.md currently mandates a dark environment — graphite well #050312, marketing field #0a0a0a — with brand magenta #D3126A and restrained violet accents. A warm-paper ground with a pine accent is a departure from it, not an interpretation of it. The one thing already sanctioned is paper: the doc names --de-paper #f7f5f2 and says explicitly not to flatten every section to one dark slab. So light ground is inside the rules; changing the accent is not. Nothing gets built until you say which way, and the brand doc gets updated in the same change rather than quietly contradicted.

01

Pine, or DE magenta used with restraint?

You wrote "DE deep pine", which is the accent I used on the reference board — so I want to check whether you were choosing it or echoing it. The magenta is not automatically guilty: what failed was magenta on a dark field, glowing. On paper, used three times a page, it is a different colour entirely. Switch the frames above and look at both before answering.

My read — Magenta on paper, held to almost nothing, keeps the brand intact and still reads as the reset. Pine is the better colour in isolation; magenta is the better decision for a company that already has a mark.

02

Is this the separate development site, or the eventual live site?

You said the scroll-driven concept is for the separate development site. If this system is only ever that, it can move faster and take more risk. If it is the intended future of digeratiexperts.com, then Direction 02 has to cover every product page and the brand doc rewrite becomes a real piece of work rather than a note.

My read — Build it as the development site, but specify it as if it will win, so nothing has to be redone if it does.

✓

Correction: the paper lockup already exists

An earlier draft of this page claimed the Digerati mark had no light-ground version and that one would have to be drawn. That was wrong. brand/digerati-logo.svg is the light-ground lockup — gold mark, graphite wordmark — and brand/README.md records that the gold was deliberately corrected from #F5E48A to #E3B23C precisely because the old value failed contrast on warm paper. Paper #F7F5F2 is a locked token and the primary lockup is drawn for it. The frames on this page now use that real asset; the earlier draft recoloured the mark, which brand/README.md forbids outright.

Closed — no work needed, and one less dependency than reported.
