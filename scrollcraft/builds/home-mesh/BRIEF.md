**Self-authored, not interviewed.** Joe (2026-10-21) asked for the eight interview
questions to be answered "based on scrolling my website for answers ... and what
Claude memories have in them about DE". The answers below are taken from:

- the live homepage copy (HomepageV8 sections 01 to 16, reused unchanged by

- the eight answers Joe approved on 2026-10-05 for `situation-gallery`

No Claude memory files are available in this cloud session, so nothing here
comes from memory. Every quoted phrase is on the live site today. Joe can
overrule any answer; the build follows the brief, so a changed answer changes

Scope rule: this is a **layer on the homepage**, not a new page. All 14 live
sections stay, in the same order, with the same words. Scrollcraft adds
movement and one signature moment; it removes nothing. Motion follows
`design/MOTION_LANGUAGE.md` (state, hierarchy, continuity, feedback; no
spectacle) and switches off under reduced motion.

   relief that a real person owns it. The moment: **an alert reaches a named

8. **Assets:** the approved card objects and page plates already on the concept,

## Grammar: chaptered editorial, as a homepage layer

Chosen because the content is fixed: the homepage must keep all 14 sections in
order, which is a chaptered document by nature, and the concept already has a
chapter pill. Why the other seven lost:

- Filmic one-shot and continuous world: contradict "distinct scenes".
- Gallery: taken by situation-gallery, and the homepage is an argument, not a catalog.
- Typographic poster: forbids cards and wastes the approved objects and portrait.
- Split stage: would force every section into two columns; content cannot move.
- Rhythmic cutlist: contradicts "calm".
- Live surface: DE's product is a service; there is no real running surface to show.
- Passage: taken by why-passage.

Chaptered editorial is already used twice (de-v2, proactive-ecosystem-amplify),
so this build has to differ from both everywhere else. See the gate below.

## Signature move: the pill becomes the principal

The glass chapter pill tracks the visitor's chapter all the way down (exists).
When the close comes into view, its chapter links fold away and the pill
becomes a single "Get a Cyber Risk Assessment" button carrying Joe's small
portrait and "Principal-led · Joseph Petro" (the site's own words: "Principal-led", "Joe stays directly involved in risk assessments"; it does not claim Joe takes every booking). Scroll back up and it unfolds into
chapters again. It is driven by the page's position, not a timer; under reduced
motion it swaps in one frame.

## The peak: the hand-off

In the monitoring section the three steps "Signal → Human triage → Named owner"
are drawn by scroll: a magenta line runs from Signal through Human triage, and
lands on Named owner, which lights. The figure says "Illustrative"; nothing
claims live telemetry.

**Peak sentence:** "An alert came in and I watched it land on an actual person."

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where the alert ends with a person's
name, and the menu ends with the founder's face.

**Authored silence:** none added. The FAQ after the peak is the calm.
