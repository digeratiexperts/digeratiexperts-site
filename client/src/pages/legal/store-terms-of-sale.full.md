# DE Store Terms of Sale

## 1. What These Terms Are and How They Fit Together

**1.1** These Store Terms of Sale ("Store Terms") apply when you buy a subscription, service, dashboard, or hardware kit from Digerati Experts, LLC ("DE") through the DE store or another DE self-serve online checkout without a signed Order Form (a "Store Purchase," as defined in the DE Master Services Agreement).

**1.2** **Governing documents.** A Store Purchase is governed by the DE Master Services Agreement version v2026.10 (the "MSA"), including **MSA Exhibit I (DE Store and Self-Serve Terms)**, together with these Store Terms, the schedule below for what you bought, the Responsibility Matrix, and the product page. Each is linked at checkout. The MSA's Order of Precedence (MSA Article 2) applies: Exhibit I controls Store Purchases where it differs from the rest of the MSA. These Store Terms and their schedules are the "plan" and "responsibility matrix for the plan" referred to in MSA Exhibit I, Section I.2. If anything in these Store Terms conflicts with MSA Exhibit I, Exhibit I controls.

**1.3** **Signed clients.** If you have a signed MSA Order Form with DE, managed services under that Order Form renew and end under MSA Sections 6.2 and 6.4, not under these Store Terms (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.3(g)).

**1.4** **Who may buy.** The DE store sells to business buyers. At checkout you confirm that you are buying for business use on behalf of the business you name and that you have authority to bind it (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.1). CONSUMER-ONLY If DE accepts an order from an individual buying for personal, family, or household use, the consumer protections in MSA Exhibit I, Section I.1, and every consumer protection law that cannot be waived apply, and nothing in these Store Terms limits them.

**1.5** **United States only.** DE ships only to street addresses in the United States and does not ship to freight forwarders or reshippers. You will not export or re-export DE products or services in violation of U.S. export or sanctions laws.

**1.6** OPTIONAL — WHERE WE SELL **Where we sell.** DE sells only to customers whose billing address and shipping address are both in a state DE supports. The supported states are listed on the DE store. DE does not currently sell to customers in {{EXCLUDED_STATES}}. The store checks both addresses and blocks an order from an unsupported state before payment. If DE learns after payment that an order does not meet this rule, DE will cancel it and refund it in full under the DE Shipping, Delay Notice, and Order Cancellation Policy. DE may add or remove supported states for future orders; a change does not affect warranty service, support, or cancellation rights on purchases already made.

## 2. Orders, Prices, Taxes, and Payment

**2.1** **Order formation.** Your order is a "paid, accepted order" when DE has received payment and all required acceptances and has sent an order confirmation (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.1). DE may decline an order before then for a stated reason, such as stock, export screening, or failed payment, and will refund any amount charged.

**2.2** **Prices.** Prices are in U.S. dollars and are shown on the product page and at checkout. Hardware, software, and services are listed as separate lines.

**2.3** **Taxes and fees.** You pay applicable sales, use, transaction privilege, and similar taxes and any government-imposed fees (for example, California's electronic waste recycling fee) as shown at checkout, unless you give DE a valid exemption certificate before checkout (MSA Section 5.3).

**2.4** **Payment.** Payment is taken on the payment processor's hosted page. DE does not receive or store full card numbers.

**2.5** **Billing questions.** Email billing@digeratiexperts.com. Legal notices and cancellation requests go to admin@digeratiexperts.com (MSA Article 18).

## 3. Subscriptions, Automatic Renewal, and One-Click Cancellation

**3.1** Store subscriptions renew automatically, monthly or annually, as shown at checkout, until you cancel. The renewal disclosures, your separate consent, the acknowledgment email, one-click online cancellation, the effect of cancellation, and the renewal reminders are in **MSA Exhibit I, Section I.3**, which is incorporated here.

**3.2** **How to cancel.** Use the "Cancel subscription" button in your DE store account, or send DE's preformatted cancellation email. You may also cancel by email to admin@digeratiexperts.com or by phone at (480) 519-5892. Cancellation stops all future renewals and charges immediately, with no early termination fee (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.3(c)–(d)).

**3.3** **New York reminder.** If your billing address is in New York and your subscription renews for a period longer than one month, DE will serve the renewal notice personally or by certified mail at least 15 and not more than 30 days before the date by which you must cancel to avoid renewal, as N.Y. General Obligations Law § 5-903 requires (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.3(e)). Because you may cancel a store subscription at any time up to renewal, that date is the renewal date.

**4.1** In-stock hardware kits ship within seven (7) calendar days after a paid, accepted order. Delays, your right to cancel, and refunds are handled under **MSA Exhibit I, Section I.4** and the DE Shipping, Delay Notice, and Order Cancellation Policy.

**4.2** Hardware sold through the DE store is sold outright and is not DE equipment. Title and risk of loss pass to you when the carrier delivers it to your shipping address (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.6). DE handles carrier claims for loss or damage in transit.

## 6. Service Models and the Tier Rule for DE Software

**6.1** Each subscription or kit is sold under one Service Model, Managed, Co-Managed, or Self-Managed, shown on the product page and at checkout (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.2). Who does what under each model is set out in the Responsibility Matrix below.

**6.2** **Tier rule.** Under the Managed and Co-Managed models, you will not disable, uninstall, or bypass security or management agents that DE deploys (MSA Article 4(e)). Under the Self-Managed model, you may remove DE software at any time using DE's published removal guide; DE's support for removed software ends when you remove it, and removing software does not cancel a subscription (MSA Article 4(e); Exhibit I, Section I.2(c)).

**6.3** **Third-party tools.** Some tools are licensed to DE by their makers for DE to deliver services. Under the Self-Managed model you may use them only as their makers' terms allow, and DE lists on the product page whether each tool stays with you, must be relicensed in your name, or is removed when your subscription ends.

**B.3** **Operating system license.** Each Windows device ships either (a) with its original manufacturer (OEM) Windows license, reinstalled only from that same PC's manufacturer recovery image, or (b) with Windows supplied through a Microsoft Authorized Refurbisher or a Third Party Refurbisher under one. The listing states which. DE does not use volume, retail-transfer, or gray-market keys.

**B.4** **Sanitization and preinstalled software.** See the DE Data Sanitization Statement and Preinstalled Software Notice. Self-Managed kit buyers may remove DE software (Section 6.2).

**B.5** **Warranty, shipping, and returns.** DE 90-Day Limited Warranty (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.5); ship-by promise and delay process (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.4); DE Return and Refund Policy.

**B.6** **Voice equipment.** If a kit includes phones, softphone licenses, or DE-provided telephone numbers, the DE Voice Services Addendum applies to those services. Before any number is activated you must (a) acknowledge the 911 Advisory in Voice Services Addendum Exhibit V-1, which describes when 911 may not work or may be limited, as 47 C.F.R. § 9.11(b)(5) requires, (b) register a Registered Location for each line under Exhibit V-2, and (c) place the 911 warning labels in Exhibit V-3 on or near each device. DE will not activate a number until (a) and (b) are recorded.

**B.7** **Excluded devices.** Kits do not include intrusion, panic, or holdup alarm devices or alarm panels. Kits are designed for self-installation; DE store kits do not include on-site installation.

**B.8** **Batteries.** DE checks battery health and condition before shipment and does not ship swollen, damaged, or recalled batteries or loose spare batteries.

## Schedule C. Standalone, Co-Managed, and Self-Managed Services and Dashboards for IT Departments

**C.1** **What it is.** Individual DE services, tools, and dashboards (for example, monitoring, endpoint security, SASE / Zero Trust access, backup, vulnerability visibility, and reporting dashboards) sold to IT departments that keep some or all operational duties in-house. Each SKU is sold as **Co-Managed** or **Self-Managed** (MSA Exhibit I, Section I.2(b)–(c)).

**C.2** **Duties.** DE and you each perform the duties the Responsibility Matrix assigns. Under Self-Managed, DE does not monitor alerts, apply patches, or respond to incidents unless the plan says so; alerts route to the contacts you name.

**C.3** **Access and accounts.** You keep your administrator credentials secure, use multi-factor authentication on DE dashboards, and promptly remove access for people who leave.

**C.4** **Acceptable use.** You will use DE tools only on systems you own or are authorized to administer, and not to monitor anyone unlawfully.

**C.5** **Data.** You are the owner of your data. DE processes it under MSA Article 7 and, where signed, the DPA, and notifies you of a Security Incident under MSA Section 7.3. You decide on and give any notices the law requires you, as data owner, to give to individuals or regulators.

**C.6** **End of subscription.** When a subscription ends, DE stops the service, makes your data available for export for thirty (30) days (MSA Section 6.4(c)), and provides instructions to remove DE software.
