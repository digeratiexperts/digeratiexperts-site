## 5. Cookies, analytics, session replay and advertising pixels

Strictly necessary cookies run always. **Analytics** (Google Analytics 4, Microsoft Clarity session replay and heatmaps) and **advertising** (Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag) load only after you choose "Accept" for that category in our cookie banner, and stop when you withdraw consent. Session replay records how the page is used (clicks, scrolling, page content); we configure it to mask form fields and do not run it on the portal, checkout or DE Desk chat. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a choice to turn off advertising cookies. Details and a list of providers: **Cookie and Tracking Notice**.

If you accept advertising cookies, information about your visits (such as pages viewed and a cookie identifier) is made available to Meta and LinkedIn to measure and target ads. Some state laws call this "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out at any time with "Cookie preferences" in the Site footer or by turning on GPC.

We record calls and create transcripts to document requests, verify instructions, train our own staff and review quality, using AI speech-to-text and analysis tools that may be provided by service providers contractually barred from using recordings or transcripts to train their own models or for any other purpose. **To opt out,** press {{OPT_OUT_KEY}} or say "do not record" at the start of the call; recording and AI analysis stop for that call and we log your choice.
