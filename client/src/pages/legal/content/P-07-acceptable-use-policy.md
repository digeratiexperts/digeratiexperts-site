This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to everyone who uses services, networks, devices, software, voice numbers or messaging provided by Digerati Experts, LLC ("DE"), including our clients, their users and store buyers using DE-managed services. It is part of your agreement with DE.

## 1. You may not use DE services to

1. Break any law, or help anyone else do so.
2. Access, scan, probe or test any system, network or account without the owner's authorization, or exceed the authorization you have (18 U.S.C. § 1030; A.R.S. § 13-2316). Security testing of your own systems is allowed only with DE's prior written coordination when DE manages them.
3. Distribute malware, run botnets, phishing pages or command-and-control servers, or mine cryptocurrency on DE-managed systems.
4. Send spam or unsolicited bulk email; send commercial email that violates CAN-SPAM (15 U.S.C. § 7704) or state law.
5. Make robocalls or send texts that violate the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227), the Telemarketing Sales Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 310), Do Not Call rules or state law; use artificial or AI-generated voices without required consent; or use an autodialer to call emergency lines, hospitals or cell phones without consent.
6. Spoof caller ID to defraud, cause harm or wrongfully obtain anything of value (Truth in Caller ID Act, 47 U.S.C. § 227(e)); misrepresent the source of a message.
7. Store, send or link to child sexual abuse material. DE reports apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as the law requires (18 U.S.C. § 2258A) and preserves it as required.
8. Infringe intellectual property, including pirated software on DE-managed devices.
9. Harass, threaten or defame anyone, or send content that promotes violence.
10. Export or re-export DE products, software or kits in violation of U.S. export or sanctions laws (15 C.F.R. Parts 730–774; 31 C.F.R. Chapter V), or provide them to sanctioned persons or countries.
11. Disable, uninstall or bypass DE security or management agents under a Managed or Co-Managed service model (self-managed buyers may remove DE software as their terms allow).
12. Resell or share DE services or accounts outside your organization without DE's written consent.
13. Interfere with DE's services or other clients, or use resources in a way that degrades them.

## 2. Voice and messaging rules

You will register text messaging campaigns as carriers require (10DLC), obtain and keep consent records, honor opt-outs by any reasonable method, provide accurate 911 location information for each line, and respond to DE within 24 hours to any traceback request about your calls.

## 3. Security responsibilities

Protect your passwords and MFA devices, tell DE immediately about a suspected incident, and do not send passwords or card numbers through tickets, chat or email.

## 4. Monitoring

DE monitors its services and the systems it manages for security, abuse and contract compliance, as described in your agreement and our Privacy Policy. You are responsible for telling your own employees that their use of your systems is monitored, where the law requires it.

## 5. Enforcement

If you violate this AUP, DE may remove content, block traffic, suspend the affected service or terminate it, as allowed by your agreement. Where possible DE gives notice first; DE may act immediately when there is an active security threat, a legal violation or abuse, and only for as long as needed (MSA § 5.6(b)). DE may report illegal activity to law enforcement.

## 6. Reporting abuse

Report abuse of DE services to admin@digeratiexperts.com with "Abuse" in the subject. Report security vulnerabilities in DE's own systems under our Vulnerability Disclosure Policy.
