## Where we use AI

| Where | What the AI does | What a person does |
|---|---|---|
| **DE Desk chat** (website and portal) | An AI assistant answers questions, gathers details and opens tickets. It is always labeled "AI assistant". | DE staff can take over at any time and confirm anything affecting your services, billing, security or contract. |
| **Homepage "Describe your situation"** | Suggests DE services that may fit what you describe. | You decide; nothing is ordered or saved to a profile unless you choose. |
| **Phone calls** | After the recording announcement, AI transcribes calls, writes summaries and grades call quality. Press {{OPT_OUT_KEY}} or say "do not record" to stop recording and AI analysis for that call. | DE reviews summaries and grades. |
| **Support and security operations** | Summarizes tickets and security alerts, drafts replies and suggests next steps. | A technician reviews and approves every action on your systems. AI never runs a change on your systems by itself. |
| **Sales and marketing content** | Helps draft proposals, emails and images. | Joseph approves what is sent. DE does not make AI-voice sales calls without your prior written consent, and any AI voice call says it is AI at the start. |

## Our commitments

1. **We tell you when you are talking to AI**, and we answer truthfully if you ask whether you are talking to a person.
2. **No training on your data.** We use AI providers ({{AI_PROVIDER_NAMING}}) only under terms that bar them from using your chats, calls or files to train or improve their own models. Where a provider will not agree, we do not send it your data.
3. **No sensitive data into chat.** Do not type passwords, card numbers, health or patient information or Social Security numbers. Card numbers are blocked automatically.
4. **No consequential decisions by AI.** We do not use AI to decide anything about you that has legal or similarly significant effects (such as employment, credit, insurance or housing).
5. **Not professional advice.** AI output is general information. It is not legal, medical, mental-health, financial, insurance or compliance advice, and it does not replace your own advisers or DE's signed agreements.
6. **If you are in crisis**, DE Desk will stop and show crisis resources. In the U.S., call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). In an emergency, call 911.
7. **AI can be wrong.** Tell us at admin@digeratiexperts.com if an AI answer was incorrect; a person will follow up.
