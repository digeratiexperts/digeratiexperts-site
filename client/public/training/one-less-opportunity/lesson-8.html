From lesson 7: what four details should you give IT when you report?

What happened and when, which device, whether you entered information, and whether you approved a login.

The pattern

Habits make the tools work.

Cybersecurity works best when everyday habits support the technical protections around you. No single product or habit prevents every incident.

Before you sign in, use your usual app or bookmark. Before you approve a prompt, confirm you started it. Before you send sensitive information, review the recipient and permissions. Before you move money, follow the verification process.

Managers have a role too: make reporting easy, support people who raise concerns, and review access when people join, change roles, or leave.

Discuss backup and recovery readiness with your IT team, including whether restores have been tested.

Habits are the layer that makes the tools work.
