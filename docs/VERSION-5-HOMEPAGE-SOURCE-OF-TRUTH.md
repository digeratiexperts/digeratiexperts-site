# Version 5 homepage: source of truth

Written 2026-09-30. Preview only at `/version-5`. Production `/` stays the live homepage. Version 4 stays frozen at `/version-4`.

This cut exists because both ten-chapter Version 4 drafts were longer than the live homepage and weaker as a front door. One dropped the person and the place. The other put the new page back inside the old chrome and invented an environment from headcount.

## Process used while building

1. Write the sentences before any component.
2. Screen one must show a person, a place, and one next step. If it has less of those than the live hero, it is not an improvement.
3. Screen two names the disconnected environment in words. No form, no diagram, no degree.
4. Screen three puts the assessment before the ways in, then stops. Proof, packages, and the desk are links.
5. Do not add a chapter whose job is to explain the previous chapter.

## What this page shows

1. **Who.** "You lead the business. We lead the technology." Joseph Petro, Founder & Chief Technology Strategist, Chandler, Arizona. The photograph is the one already published on the live homepage. One action: Understand Your Environment, opening `/book`. The phone is `PRIMARY_PHONE`.
2. **The gap.** People, accounts, computers, mail, sites, and vendors can all be in place and still leave no one holding the whole environment.
3. **The way in.** The assessment comes before a recommendation. Then three doors: Handle our IT (ProActive and co-managed), solve a business need, and the client marketplace for someone already a client. Team, Client Bill of Rights, and Trust Center are links.

## What this page refuses

- A second navigation, a second assessment name, or a headcount form.
- A degree instrument, a designer caption, or a new layout per chapter.
- Prices, vendor names, invented reviews, monitoring hours, and the "only firm" claim.
- Replacing `/`.

## Acceptance

- One `h1`. Native scroll. `noindex` via `VersionFrame`. Absent from the sitemap and from normal navigation.
- The first screen includes the founder photograph, Chandler, the phone, and the booking action.
- No horizontal overflow at 390, 768, and 1440.
