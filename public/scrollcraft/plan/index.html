DE Scrollcraft Experience Plan

Scrollcraft is a storytelling capability, not a visual theme. DE looks like DE whether a page scrolls cinematically or sits perfectly still. This document decides where the capability belongs, what each experience must accomplish, what materials it needs, and how those materials get made — before another line of page code is written.

What DE is. A Managed Security Service Provider and an IT Managed Services company. Managed security and managed IT are the offering every page sells; the flagships are built around them and they are developed first, to the highest finish. DE also does AI and the rest of its fourteen domains, each within its scope. AI material is wanted, but AI is one contained domain and never the lead: Automation + AI stays as the last flagship, built only after the four service flagships and never given more prominence in the information architecture than managed security and managed IT. Set at Joe's direction, 2026-09-01.

01

Four experience levels, with a motion budget each

Every route on the site gets exactly one designation. The level decides how much motion a page is even allowed to request, which stops "impressive everywhere" before it starts.

Levels and their hard motion budgets
LevelPurposeMotion budget (maximum)
A · FULLChange how the visitor understands the company1 signature behavior · 1 major peak · 2–4 supporting devices · deliberate quiet sections · designed reduced-motion parity
B · SELECTIVESequence or a visual explanation earns its place1 meaningful scroll sequence · 1 secondary reveal system
C · NORMALRead, compare, decideEntrances, state changes, small hierarchy cues only
D · UTILITYGet something done quicklyFunctional feedback only. Motion gets out of the way.

Level A pages additionally require an approved asset brief (§5) and a fingerprint check against the registry before any code. Levels C and D never receive Scrollcraft treatment — the existing DE design system carries them.

05

Material creation plan

Materials are produced as planned sets against approved briefs — never one prompt, capture, or texture at a time. Three programs, in priority order:

1 · The DE diagram system (Class C — the signature)

One governed visual language for architecture, workflow, protection-boundary, assessment-finding, coverage, and lifecycle diagrams. Designed once as a static system on DE tokens — node shapes, line weights, label conventions in Oxanium, magenta reserved for the active path, violet for illumination — then given an animation grammar so the same diagram works still on a Level C page and alive inside a Level A experience. This is how Scrollcraft and the normal site genuinely mesh, and it becomes a brand signature no competitor can copy cheaply.

2 · The concept art set (Class D — eight pieces, one material family)

Conceived together, art-directed against the four flagship stories before any page is built: Fragmentation · Connection · Boundary · Continuity · Orchestration · Intelligence · Recovery · Human oversight. Concept, not noun — no laptops for managed IT, no shields for risk, no padlocked clouds for backup. Production path: generated base material where the network allowlist permits (api.kie.ai + kieai.redpandaai.co — currently blocked), composited and finished with code/vector overlays in the DE palette; authored generatively in-house if the allowlist stays closed. Every piece reproducible and documented. The board test from Phase 3 applies: laid out together, the stills must read as a premium DE campaign before anything moves.

3 · Photography + evidence capture (Classes B and A)

  • Photography brief (a half-day shoot, one photographer): the Chandler office, working environment, hands-on engineering, equipment and deployments where appropriate, meeting moments, founder in context. No AI-generated humans presented as DE staff — ever.
  • Evidence capture program: sanitized captures of DE Desk, the client portal, the solution builder, an assessment deliverable's structure, a roadmap view. Sanitization checklist per capture; each asset carries provenance in the ledger.
  • Class E sourcing: only where reality beats creation (Arizona landscape, architecture); single-voice coherence per surface.

06

Four flagship creative briefs

Phase-2 direction for the first four Level A experiences. Each carries a deliberately different fingerprint; none may reuse the chaptered-editorial + margin-map skeleton.

1 · Homepage — The Environment Wakes Up, rebuilt

Route: / · replaces the current homepage only when it clears every gate

Purpose
Change what the visitor believes DE is: from "an IT vendor" to "the company that makes the whole environment work as one system."
Audience
Arizona owner-operators and office managers in law, CPA, medical, real estate, veterinary — pressed for time, allergic to vendor theater.
Belief before → after
"IT providers are interchangeable ticket-takers" → "My environment could work as one designed system, and DE is who does that."
Energy curve
calm → unease → awe (peak) → agency → confidence → warmth → resolve. Proven in Version B; retained.
Signature + peak
The transformation moment — a fragmented business environment becoming coherent — redesigned from scratch on the new diagram system and the Fragmentation/Connection materials. Explicitly not the existing environment map, not a margin folio. Candidate mechanisms explored in Phase 4 storyboards.
Quiet moment
The authored silence after the evidence field survives the rebuild.
Materials
Asset table in §5. Salvaged from Version B: sourced facts with citations, dossier content, real photography, DE Desk capture, canonical CTA.
Mobile
A designed five-moment sequence (arrival, fracture, transformation, proof, close) — not the desktop compressed.
Fingerprint
Must clear both registry rows. Chaptered editorial + margin map is unavailable by rule.

2 · ProActive Ecosystem — a living operating cadence

Route: /solutions/proactive-ecosystem · replaces the #166 treatment when ready

Purpose
Make "operated, not watched" tangible: technology becoming an operating system over time.
Belief before → after
"A plan is a bundle of tools" → "A plan is a cadence that runs my environment: designed before defended, monitored before it breaks, measured to a roadmap."
Signature + peak
A living cadence — Observe → Decide → Protect → Improve — cycling as the visitor scrolls, with services surfacing at the moment they become operationally relevant. Time and rhythm as the grammar, not spatial assembly. Peak: one full revolution completing with the environment measurably better than the revolution before.
Quiet moment
The published rates, still and exact.
Materials
Orchestration + Continuity concept pieces; cadence diagrams from the diagram system; sanitized roadmap/QBR structure as Class A evidence.
Mobile
The cadence as a vertical rhythm; one cycle, larger type, no lateral travel.
Fingerprint
Distinct from homepage by construction: cyclical/temporal vs transformational/spatial.

3 · How We Protect — layered, adaptive, continuous

Route: open decision — elevate /solutions/unified-security or create dedicated

Purpose
Replace the abstract word "cybersecurity" with a visible mechanism: protection as layers around a real business.
Belief before → after
"Security is a product you buy" → "Security is layers that adapt and never stop: identity, endpoint, network, email, backup, security operations."
Signature + peak
A recognizable business environment progressively enclosed by protective boundaries, each layer visibly answering a named threat from the evidence register. Peak: an attack path attempted against the finished enclosure, failing layer by layer. No SOC dashboard, no neon wall, no fake live telemetry — the governance forbids illustrative telemetry presented as real.
Quiet moment
The 45-day Arizona notification statute, typeset plainly.
Materials
Boundary + Recovery concept pieces; protection-boundary diagrams; cited industry facts (existing Class E).
Mobile
Layers accumulate as full-viewport states; the attack-path moment simplified to three beats.
Fingerprint
Enclosure/containment grammar — distinct from both transformation (home) and cadence (ProActive).

4 · Cyber Risk Assessment — unknown to roadmap

Route: /assessment · the conversion flagship

Purpose
Make the assessment itself understandable so sales copy doesn't have to carry it. The page is the product demo.
Belief before → after
"An assessment is a sales pitch in disguise" → "It's a real inspection with findings I can act on with anyone — and the obvious first step."
Signature + peak
A deliberately incomplete environment gets progressively inspected: observed, annotated, classified, prioritized — findings acquiring severity and order as the visitor scrolls. Peak: the prioritized findings resolving into a roadmap. Annotation/inspection grammar; the visitor watches rigor happen.
Quiet moment
"No obligation. You hear back within one business day." — the real promises, still.
Materials
Intelligence + Fragmentation concept pieces; assessment-finding diagrams; sanitized deliverable structure as Class A evidence.
Mobile
Inspection beats as discrete cards acquiring classification; single column, thumb-paced.
Fingerprint
Forensic/annotative — the only page where the interface idiom is examination.

Automation + AI, last. AI material is wanted, and it is built only after the four service flagships: manual work → orchestration → autonomous workflow → human oversight, handoffs collapsing into one path with a human approval gate still lit, framed as one of the fourteen domains a managed security and managed IT company operates. No robots, no AI blobs, and never more prominence in the IA than the security and managed IT pages. Brief 5.

08

Standing rules the program inherits

  • The brand foundation is locked: #050312 · Warm Paper · #D3126A · restrained violet illumination · Space Grotesk · Inter · Oxanium. Scrollcraft consumes the DE system; it never replaces it.
  • Truthfulness: no fabricated certifications, partnerships, customer counts, testimonials, SLAs, or telemetry. Sourced statistics carry source and year, as industry context only. The empty reviews catalog stays empty until reality fills it.
  • Evidence hierarchy governs every surface: real artifact → real data → real person → explanatory diagram → sanitized interface → illustration → photography → atmosphere → icon.
  • The fingerprint registry is binding. Chaptered editorial + margin map is occupied twice and closed. Every new build clears every existing row.
  • MERGED and LIVE are separate states. Every release names both, is verified against production, and ships with its rollback path. Guardrail expectations (the homepage smoke test) are updated with a release, never disabled.
  • Levels C and D never receive Scrollcraft. Contact stays beautifully boring; checkout stays almost invisible.
  • Joe holds merge, deploy, and approval authority at every gate. This plan itself is inert until approved.
